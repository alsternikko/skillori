Changelog

What’s new

Every version of Skillori and what it brought along, newest first. The download button always hands you the latest one.

v0.2.17

21 Nov 2026

Add again for Codex plugins now only adds from the catalog Codex moved to, and plugins from that catalog are no longer marked as not loaded when Codex can't reach it. The chips on an app's page say what they mean.

v0.2.16

20 Nov 2026

When you unlink a source that stopped answering, Skillori remembers it and what it said, on the skill's page and in Find sources.

v0.2.15

20 Nov 2026

When a source has moved and leaves a note, the skill page shows what it says, with a button to the new address.

v0.2.14

20 Nov 2026

A skill with no known source can look for one from its own page, next to Link.

v0.2.13

20 Nov 2026

After an update, a skill's page keeps the result in view, and a moved skill says where it came from.

v0.2.12

20 Nov 2026

Skills whose source is gone can be moved to where they live now, or kept as a local copy. A skill page now stays put after an update.

v0.2.11

19 Nov 2026

When a source can't be reached, the library now says which one, how many skills it affects, and when Skillori asks again, and after an update it says the update went through. An update now asks only its own skills' source, and Skillori remembers GitHub's answers across restarts, so it is asked to wait far less often.

v0.2.10

19 Nov 2026

The menu bar panel no longer shakes when it changes height. Opening a source's list, closing it, and the lines an update prints now grow and shrink the panel smoothly, with its top edge staying put under the menu bar.

v0.2.9

19 Nov 2026

Find sources can now finish the job: Link and update has npx skills put the source's newer version in place, and from then on those skills update like any other. Folders and Check now also work on the library's front page.

v0.2.8

19 Nov 2026

Skills that showed up without a return address can now find one: Find sources looks up where they came from and tells you when your copy is behind.

v0.2.7

19 Nov 2026

A skill that its author shares as an install script on their own site can now be linked as its source. Paste the script's address, or the whole curl command, under Link…; Skillori reads which files the script would write, without running it, and tells you when they change.

v0.2.6

19 Nov 2026

Skillori can now open when you log in. Turn on Open at login under General in Settings; macOS keeps the choice, so it also shows under Login Items in System Settings.

v0.2.5

19 Nov 2026

Things for you now only lists what Skillori can actually do. Newer Cursor plugin versions are shown on Cursor's page as information, and Codex plugins that Codex stopped reading after moving them to a new catalog can be added again with one click.

v0.2.4

18 Nov 2026

Update no longer promises what it can’t deliver. Skillori now reads a Claude Code plugin’s version the way Claude Code does, so new files under the same version aren’t called an update. And when an update ends without changing anything, the line tells you why instead of quietly saying Update again.

v0.2.3

17 Nov 2026

The library now opens on what needs you, one sentence and one button for each thing. Your own skills and your apps' skills each have a door of their own, and every skill and app has a page that says what to do.

v0.2.2

16 Nov 2026

Skillori now checks Cursor and Codex plugins too, and tells you which app to update them in. It also learned to count: an older version at the source is no longer called an update.

v0.2.1

16 Nov 2026

Skillori now rests when you aren’t looking: the light in its panel stops when the panel is closed, so it no longer uses your CPU in the background. Faint text is easier to read, and VoiceOver says more about what it reads.

v0.2.0

16 Nov 2026

Skillori now watches your skill folders, sends a notification when a source has something new, and checks Claude Code plugins too. Before you update, it shows which files will change and which of them can run, and it no longer credits you with agents you don’t have.

v0.1.2

16 Nov 2026

Skillori now finds the skills the Claude app installs through plugins, skills from more agents, and skill folders that have moved. If it told you that you had zero skills, it was wrong, and it’s sorry.

v0.1.1

15 Nov 2026

The Library window stops at a sensible size now, instead of folding itself into a postage stamp.

v0.1.0

14 Nov 2026

First beta. Skillori finds the agent skills on your Mac, works out where each one came from, and tells you when the original has moved on. Update a skill from its source in one click, link the ones it can’t place, and from now on Skillori keeps itself up to date too.
